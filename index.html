QBANK Clipper

QBANK Clipper is the desktop tool that QBANK Clipz (run by Sandor Hernandez-Amador, QBANK Capital) uses to turn moments from live game streams into short vertical videos and publish them to QBANK Clipz's own accounts.

What it does

  • Finds highlight moments in a stream and cuts them into 9:16 clips (facecam above gameplay, captions, credit to the streamer).
  • Runs quality and safety checks on every clip before it can be published (1080p60, audio/video sync, slurs bleeped, positive or funny moments only).
  • Publishes finished clips through each platform's official API: YouTube Shorts (YouTube Data API), Instagram Reels (Instagram Platform API) and TikTok (TikTok Content Posting API, once approved).
  • Shows upload history and post analytics for those accounts.

How it works with TikTok

The account owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes QBANK Clipper. QBANK Clipper then either sends a finished clip to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft, which the owner edits and posts inside the TikTok app, or, on the "Post to TikTok" screen, posts it with the title, privacy, interaction and disclosure settings the owner picks there. QBANK Clipper does not read TikTok videos, comments, followers or messages.
