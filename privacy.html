Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

QBANK Clipper is operated by QBANK Capital (Sandor Hernandez-Amador). It is used to publish QBANK Clipz's own short videos to QBANK Clipz's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not sold to or used by other people.

What data QBANK Clipper handles

  • Google / YouTube: sign-in tokens, the channel ID and name, and the IDs and statistics of videos QBANK Clipper uploaded.
  • Instagram (Meta): sign-in tokens, the Instagram account ID and username, and the IDs and insights of Reels QBANK Clipper published.
  • TikTok: the TikTok open ID, display name and profile picture link (scope user.info.basic), the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues, the permissions granted (video.upload, and video.publish if Direct Post is used), the creator settings TikTok returns before a post (nickname, allowed privacy options, whether comments/duets/stitches are allowed, maximum video length), and the publish IDs and status of videos QBANK Clipper sent.
  • Videos and captions that QBANK Clipper makes and sends to these platforms.

QBANK Clipper does not collect data about viewers, followers, comments or messages, and does not use any data for advertising.

How it is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload the owner's videos, show their upload status, and show statistics for those videos.
  • Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computers, in files readable only by the owner, and are never shared or published. Tokens are sent only to the platform that issued them.
  • No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone else.
  • Data is kept while the account stays connected. Disconnecting an account in QBANK Clipper deletes its stored tokens.

How to revoke access

  • Google: Google Account permissions
  • Instagram: Instagram Settings → Apps and websites (or Accounts Center → Apps and websites)
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions → QBANK Clipper → Remove access

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions or deletion requests: qbankcapital@gmail.com.
