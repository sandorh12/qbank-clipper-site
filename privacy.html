Privacy Policy

QBANK Clipper is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no third-party users.

The tool accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account, through Google and Meta OAuth, to upload videos and read basic channel information.

Sign-in tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold or shared with anyone else.

How to revoke access

You can revoke access at any time:

  • Google: Google Account permissions
  • Instagram: Instagram Settings → Apps and websites (or Accounts Center → Apps and websites)
